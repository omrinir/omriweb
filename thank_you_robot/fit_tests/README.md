# Fit tests (print at 100 %)

Small pieces cut from the real parts. Print them before the full robot. If something is too tight or too loose, tell me the part and I'll adjust the model.

| File | Test with | It's right when |
|---|---|---|
| `arm_servo_pad.stl` | an SG90 | The servo's top slides into the pocket, the round boss pokes through the hole, and the tabs sit flat over the 2 screw holes |
| `head_servo_plate.stl` | an SG90 | The servo pushes up into the rectangle from the underside and the tabs line up with the 2 screw holes |
| `nano_rails.stl` | the Nano | The board slides along the two grooves without forcing and doesn't rattle |
| `sign_slot.stl` + `sign_tab.stl` | each other | The tab pushes into the slot with light friction |
| `arm_horn.stl` | a single-arm SG90 horn | The horn presses into the pocket and the screw hole lines up with the center |
| `head_horn.stl` | a single-arm SG90 horn | Same check, for the slot under the head |

`../stl_50pct/` has the whole robot at half size. It's only for checking the look. No electronics fit at that size and the gaps between parts are half as wide, so expect to sand or glue it.

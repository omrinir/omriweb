# THEY LEARN: the story

This is the story bible for the whole game (levels 1 to 63). It makes the earlier levels fit together and plans the rest.
Levels 1 to 57 are built, 58 to 60 are being built now, and 61 to 63 are planned.

## The one-line version

The dead learn from dying. Every zombie you kill teaches the rest how you did it. The better you are, the faster they learn.

## Why they learn: ECHO

- **What the infection is.** The infection is called **ECHO**. It is not a plain virus. It is a fungus-like net that grows through the brain, inspired by the cordyceps in *The Girl with All the Gifts*, the thinking cells of *Blood Music* and the hive in *Blindsight*.
- **How it teaches.** When an infected body dies, the net bursts into a cloud of spores. The spores carry the last few seconds the dead one saw and felt. Every zombie that breathes them in inherits that memory.
  - One zombie dies to a headshot, so the next ones learn to duck.
  - A hundred die to fire, so the next ones learn to wet their skin or wear ice.
- **Why it matters in play.** This is why every level brings new zombies and why each one is an answer to how you killed them before. It is also why the HUD warns **"THEY'RE LEARNING YOU"**, since the game really does track how you play (`ai/player_memory.gd`).
- **Where it came from.** ECHO was built on purpose. **Project ECHO** was a military program at a research station under the Patagonian ice, the **Glacier Lab** (level 60). Its goal was soldiers who share combat memory: if one dies, the whole squad knows what killed him.

## Who the hero is

- **The squad.** The hero was **Subject Zero**, the first of seven soldiers linked by ECHO.
- **The accident.** The Amazon field test went wrong ("Where it started", region 1). The link spread from the squad into everyone it touched, living or dead.
- **The memory wipe.** Before the hero turned, someone injected him with a suppressant called **LETHE**. It cut him out of the network and erased his memory.
  - Level 1: "We've met before." / "I don't remember." / "Good. **Then it worked.**"
- **Why it matters.** The hero is the only infected human who is not linked. He is the only one the hive cannot read, which is also why he is the only one who can hurt it.

## The STRANGER

- **Who he is.** The STRANGER is another of the seven. He is the one who gave the hero LETHE, and he chose to stay linked.
- **What he does.** He hears the hive from inside, and he walks next to the hero to steer him south toward the Glacier Lab.
- **What he means.**
  - Level 17: "the real ones cast a shadow. You don't have a shadow." He is half-real, half a voice of the net.
  - Level 54: "You're the only one of us... who **forgot**."
- **What nobody knows yet.** He cannot attack the hive himself, because a linked mind cannot attack the hive. He needs the hero to do it, and he has not told the hero that the hive wants the hero too.

## Why they go south

- **What they want.**
  - The hive wants to reach the **ROOT**, the first culture of ECHO, still alive in the Glacier Lab.
  - It also wants the hero, its missing first voice, Subject Zero. He carries the original, unmutated pattern.
  - With the ROOT and the hero together, every infected mind on the continent becomes one mind.
- **They don't want to eat him. They want him to come home.**

## The story so far, as lessons

| Region | Lesson | What it means in the story |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Northern Amazon | they only hunger | the net is young; the dead are animals |
| 2 Northeast | they learned to wait | first patience, ambushes |
| 3 Central Plateau | they learned to hunt in packs | the net links nearby bodies |
| 4 Andes | they learned to hide | camouflage, fake corpses, mirages |
| 5 Southeast | they learned our machines | they copy what killed them: cars, guns, uniforms |
| 6 Southern Cone | they learned to plan | **THE ARCHITECT**, ECHO's lead scientist, infected himself to lead them; living traitors (QUISLINGS) serve him |
| 7 Patagonia | they learned to think | the Architect is dead (54) and it did not matter: **they no longer need a leader** |

The Architect's death at level 54 is the turn of the story. Killing the head of a herd always worked before. Now the dead keep going, because the lesson has spread to all of them. "THE REST ALREADY CROSSED."

## Region 7: Patagonia, "They learned to think"

There is no leader any more. The hive thinks as one, and it starts to show it. Shapes form in flocks, the same words come from many mouths, and it works with the wind and the cold.

### 55 WIND STEPPE: "They learned the wind"

- **Setting.** Empty steppe, sheep ranches (estancias), wind turbines and wire fences. Gales blow all the time.
- **Lesson.** The dead learned that the hero is slow to look up, and that the wind can carry them. They now come from the sky and rolling across the ground.
- **New zombies.**
  - **SHEARER.** A ranch sheep-shearer with blade shears who hides inside a flock of sheep. The boss is a giant shearer with hydraulic shears.
  - **DRIFTER (flyer).** Hangs under a torn paraglider and rides the gusts. It cannot fly against the wind, and when the wind drops it falls. Shoot the canopy and it crashes.
  - **TUMBLER.** Curled inside a ball of thorny bush, it rolls downwind fast, then opens and grabs.
- **Story scene (STRANGER).** He tells the hero the truth about the spores: "Every one you kill... teaches the rest." This is the first time the game says out loud why they learn.

### 56 ICE FJORD: "They learned the cold"

- **Setting.** A fjord with calving glacier walls, ice floes and frozen fishing boats.
- **Lesson.** Heads break, so they armor them. Bullets break ice slower than skulls. They also learned that the cold keeps them from rotting.
- **New zombies.**
  - **THAWED.** Frozen inside a shell of ice that cracks off in layers. Fire melts it at once. The boss is a giant inside a chunk of glacier.
  - **BUOY (flyer).** Lifted by a bunch of orange fishing buoys and weather balloons, drifting over the water. Each popped balloon drops it lower, and when all are gone it falls.
  - **SEALER.** Wears seal skin and swims under the ice, then bursts up out of fishing holes.

### 57 PENGUIN COAST: "They learned to be many"

- **Setting.** A penguin colony, beaches, an old lighthouse and a whaling-station shore.
- **Lesson.** A body can be shot, so they stop needing one body. The hive learned to think across many small bodies at once.
- **New zombies.**
  - **WADDLER.** Shuffles inside the huddling penguin colony. You can't shoot into the crowd without hitting penguins, so it gets close. The boss is a giant WADDLER (the BEACHMASTER).
  - **MURMUR (flyer).** An infected flock of seabirds that flies in the shape of a man. Shooting scatters birds off it, and it re-forms smaller. It is a flying zombie with no body, which is the region's idea in one enemy.
  - **BLUBBER.** A bloated zombie in an elephant-seal hide that charges belly-first along the sand.
- **Story scene (THE CHORUS).** For the first time the hive speaks: the MURMUR flock talks with many voices at once. "We remember you. You were the first of us. Come home." The hero learns that they are not hunting him to kill him.

### 58 WHALE GRAVEYARD: "They learned to wear the dead"

- **Setting.** An abandoned whaling station on a grey beach: whale skeletons, flensing platforms, rusted tryworks and oil tanks.
- **Lesson.** The spores carried thousands of headshot deaths, so the hive learned that skulls break and whale bone does not.
  It also learned that ECHO can grow in any dead thing, not only in people, and the dead here are enormous.
- **New zombies.**
  - **FLENSER.** A station worker with a flensing knife on a pole and a whale-bone shield. It moves the shield to where you aim.
    The boss is THE BONE KING, a giant with a whale skull for a helmet.
  - **SKUA (flyer).** Stitched-on wings of baleen and seal skin. It dives like a seabird and climbs away. Shoot a wing and it spirals
    down. After a few dives get shot, it starts to feint.
  - **BONECRAWLER.** Several dead fused inside a whale rib cage, walking it like a giant bone centipede. The ribs stop bullets.
- **Story.** The station has become an ECHO farm: spore-covered carcasses and hive marks on the tanks. The hive is growing new bodies.

### 59 BLACK FOREST: "They learned to burn"

- **Setting.** A burnt beech forest in Tierra del Fuego: black trunks, ash, fire lines, smoke, beaver dams.
- **Lesson.** The hero burned thousands of them, and every burning death breathed out what fire feels like. So they wear ash and
  charcoal, walk through flames, hide in smoke, and find the hero by the flash of his gun.
- **New zombies.**
  - **CHARRED.** Charcoal crust that fire cannot hurt. Shots crack it, and a cracked one takes much more damage. It rolls in the ash
    to re-crust. The boss is THE PYRE, a walking bonfire of fused bodies and burning logs.
  - **ASHMOTH (flyer).** Moth wings of burnt bark and skin. It flies to the hero's muzzle flash and loses him when he stops shooting.
    It learns to circle the last place it saw the flash.
  - **DAMBUILDER.** Wears beaver pelts and builds log barricades in front of the hero. It learned cover from him.
- **Story scene (THE STRANGER, last time).** He admits he is the one who gave the hero LETHE. The hero was the first voice of the
  hive; if he went home, no one would be left who isn't them. He points to the Glacier Lab, where it started, and fades into
  the smoke. A linked mind cannot follow where the hero is going.

### 60 GLACIER LAB: "They learned where they came from"

- **Setting.** Project ECHO's station inside the glacier: ice tunnels, labs, broken cryo tanks, blast doors, red alarms. ECHO fungus
  covers the walls and pulses toward the deep levels (the ROOT).
- **Lesson.** The hive learned where it was born and now guards its cradle. It learned the lab's own systems (doors, lockdowns)
  and, from the files, the hero's own combat pattern.
- **New zombies.**
  - **SPECIMEN.** Escaped test subjects, some thawing out of cryo tanks as you pass. They copy the hero's last move. The boss is
    **SUBJECT FOUR**, one of the seven ECHO soldiers, who fights with the hero's own moves.
  - **BLOOM (flyer).** A floating ECHO colony like a glowing jellyfish. It barely attacks: it **teaches**. Zombies under it learn
    on the spot. This is the learning made visible in one enemy. Kill it first.
  - **WARDEN.** Lab security with riot armour and keycards. They lock blast doors behind the hero and run ahead to lock the next.
- **Story scene (SUBJECT ZERO).** At a terminal the hero finds his own file: original pattern, LETHE administered, "Do not let him
  go home." The hive answers through the speakers: "Welcome home."

### Planned: 61 to 63

- **61 End of the World:** Ushuaia. The hive cuts every road; the whole region moves as one body toward the lab.
- **62 The Hive Mind:** the ROOT, under the lab. Everything the hive has learned from the hero, used against him at once.
- **63 They Learned:** the ending. Does the hero destroy the ROOT, or join it? This is open for the user to decide.

## How the game shows that they learn (rules for every new zombie)

1. **Each new zombie is an answer to the hero.** Its intro and its "learns" behavior say what it learned and from what. Examples are "IT FEINTS NOW" and "THEY DUCK NOW".
2. **Each zombie also learns during the level.** After you beat its trick a few times, it changes the trick. This rule has been used since level 46.
3. **Spores at death.** From Patagonia on, a kill can release a faint glowing spore puff that drifts to the nearest zombie. That zombie gets a short "learned" flash. This is a visual tell of the lesson passing on, and it is cheap and particle-light.
4. **Level titles** are always "THEY LEARNED ...".

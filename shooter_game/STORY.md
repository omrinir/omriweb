# THEY LEARN: the story

This is the story bible for the whole game (levels 1 to 63). It makes all the levels fit together.
All 63 levels are built. The ending is the player's choice (DESTROY or JOIN, level 63).

## The one-line version

The dead learn from dying. Every zombie you kill teaches the rest how you did it. The better you are, the faster they learn.

## Why they learn: ECHO

- **What the infection is.** The infection is called **ECHO**. It is not a plain virus. It is a fungus-like net that grows through the brain, inspired by the cordyceps in *The Girl with All the Gifts*, the thinking cells of *Blood Music* and the hive in *Blindsight*.
- **How it teaches.** When an infected body dies, the net bursts into a cloud of spores. The spores carry the last few seconds the dead one saw and felt. Every zombie that breathes them in inherits that memory.
  - One zombie dies to a headshot, so the next ones learn to duck.
  - A hundred die to fire, so the next ones learn to wet their skin or wear ice.
- **Why it matters in play.** This is why every level brings new zombies and why each one is an answer to how you killed them before. It is also why the HUD warns **"THEY'RE LEARNING YOU"**, since the game really does track how you play (`ai/player_memory.gd`).
- **Where it came from.** ECHO was built on purpose. **Project ECHO** was a military program at a research station under the Patagonian ice, the **Glacier Lab** (level 60). Its goal was soldiers who share combat memory: if one dies, the whole squad knows what killed him.
- **Why it learns HIM (the answer, levels 62-63).** Project ECHO was built to learn one man, Subject Zero, and copy his combat pattern into seven soldiers. When it escaped it kept doing the one thing it was made for. Every zombie since level 1 is the experiment still running: each death sends its lesson home, and the hive gets better at being him. They are not learning to beat him. They are learning to BECOME him. The ROOT, the original culture, needs the original pattern to finish ("every mind becomes one"). The hive says it to his face in level 62: "We were made to learn you. You are our teacher."

## Who the hero is

- **The squad.** The hero was **Subject Zero**, the first of seven soldiers linked by ECHO.
- **The accident.** The Amazon field test went wrong ("Where it started", region 1). The link spread from the squad into everyone it touched, living or dead.
- **The memory wipe.** Before the hero turned, someone injected him with a suppressant called **LETHE**. It cut him out of the network and erased his memory.
  - Level 1: "We've met before." / "I don't remember." / "Good. **Then it worked.**"
- **Why it matters.** The hero is the only infected human who is not linked. He is the only one the hive cannot read, which is also why he is the only one who can hurt it.

## The STRANGER

- **Who he is.** The STRANGER is another of the seven. He is the one who gave the hero LETHE, and he chose to stay linked.
- **What he does.** He hears the hive from inside, and he walks next to the hero to steer him south toward the Glacier Lab.
- **What he means.**
  - Level 17: "the real ones cast a shadow. You don't have a shadow." He is half-real, half a voice of the net.
  - Level 54: "You're the only one of us... who **forgot**."
- **What nobody knows yet.** He cannot attack the hive himself, because a linked mind cannot attack the hive. He needs the hero to do it, and he has not told the hero that the hive wants the hero too.

## Why they go south

- **What they want.**
  - The hive wants to reach the **ROOT**, the first culture of ECHO, still alive in the Glacier Lab.
  - It also wants the hero, its missing first voice, Subject Zero. He carries the original, unmutated pattern.
  - With the ROOT and the hero together, every infected mind on the continent becomes one mind.
- **They don't want to eat him. They want him to come home.**

## The story so far, as lessons

| Region | Lesson | What it means in the story |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Northern Amazon | they only hunger | the net is young; the dead are animals |
| 2 Northeast | they learned to wait | first patience, ambushes |
| 3 Central Plateau | they learned to hunt in packs | the net links nearby bodies |
| 4 Andes | they learned to hide | camouflage, fake corpses, mirages |
| 5 Southeast | they learned our machines | they copy what killed them: cars, guns, uniforms |
| 6 Southern Cone | they learned to plan | **THE ARCHITECT**, ECHO's lead scientist, infected himself to lead them; living traitors (QUISLINGS) serve him |
| 7 Patagonia | they learned to think | the Architect is dead (54) and it did not matter: **they no longer need a leader** |

The Architect's death at level 54 is the turn of the story. Killing the head of a herd always worked before. Now the dead keep going, because the lesson has spread to all of them. "THE REST ALREADY CROSSED."

## Region 7: Patagonia, "They learned to think"

There is no leader any more. The hive thinks as one, and it starts to show it. Shapes form in flocks, the same words come from many mouths, and it works with the wind and the cold.

### 55 WIND STEPPE: "They learned the wind"

- **Setting.** Empty steppe, sheep ranches (estancias), wind turbines and wire fences. Gales blow all the time.
- **Lesson.** The dead learned that the hero is slow to look up, and that the wind can carry them. They now come from the sky and rolling across the ground.
- **New zombies.**
  - **SHEARER.** A ranch sheep-shearer with blade shears who hides inside a flock of sheep. The boss is a giant shearer with hydraulic shears.
  - **DRIFTER (flyer).** Hangs under a torn paraglider and rides the gusts. It cannot fly against the wind, and when the wind drops it falls. Shoot the canopy and it crashes.
  - **TUMBLER.** Curled inside a ball of thorny bush, it rolls downwind fast, then opens and grabs.
- **Story scene (STRANGER).** He tells the hero the truth about the spores: "Every one you kill... teaches the rest." This is the first time the game says out loud why they learn.

### 56 ICE FJORD: "They learned the cold"

- **Setting.** A fjord with calving glacier walls, ice floes and frozen fishing boats.
- **Lesson.** Heads break, so they armor them. Bullets break ice slower than skulls. They also learned that the cold keeps them from rotting.
- **New zombies.**
  - **THAWED.** Frozen inside a shell of ice that cracks off in layers. Fire melts it at once. The boss is a giant inside a chunk of glacier.
  - **BUOY (flyer).** Lifted by a bunch of orange fishing buoys and weather balloons, drifting over the water. Each popped balloon drops it lower, and when all are gone it falls.
  - **SEALER.** Wears seal skin and swims under the ice, then bursts up out of fishing holes.

### 57 PENGUIN COAST: "They learned to be many"

- **Setting.** A penguin colony, beaches, an old lighthouse and a whaling-station shore.
- **Lesson.** A body can be shot, so they stop needing one body. The hive learned to think across many small bodies at once.
- **New zombies.**
  - **WADDLER.** Shuffles inside the huddling penguin colony. You can't shoot into the crowd without hitting penguins, so it gets close. The boss is a giant WADDLER (the BEACHMASTER).
  - **MURMUR (flyer).** An infected flock of seabirds that flies in the shape of a man. Shooting scatters birds off it, and it re-forms smaller. It is a flying zombie with no body, which is the region's idea in one enemy.
  - **BLUBBER.** A bloated zombie in an elephant-seal hide that charges belly-first along the sand.
- **Story scene (THE CHORUS).** For the first time the hive speaks: the MURMUR flock talks with many voices at once. "We remember you. You were the first of us. Come home." The hero learns that they are not hunting him to kill him.

### 58 WHALE GRAVEYARD: "They learned to wear the dead"

- **Setting.** PUERTO HUESO, a whaling station abandoned since 1911, on a grey shingle beach under a low sun. Whale
  skeletons and rib arches, a flensing platform, rusted oil tanks and try-pots, harpoon cannons, a catcher boat on the
  slipway and the whalers' cemetery.
- **Lesson.** The spores carried thousands of headshot deaths, so the hive learned that skulls break and whale bone does not.
  So they wear bone. It also learned that ECHO can grow in any dead thing, not only in people, and the dead here are
  enormous.
- **New zombies.**
  - **FLENSER.** A station worker with a flensing knife on a pole and a whale shoulder blade as a shield. You learn to shoot
    under it, so it learns to move the shield to where you aim. The boss is **THE BONE KING**, a giant with a whale skull
    for a helmet and a jawbone for a shield. In the fight he learns to guard his back and to read your aim.
  - **SKUA (flyer).** Stitched-on wings of baleen and seal skin. It soars, folds and stoops like the coast's skuas, then
    climbs away. Shoot a wing and it spirals down. After a few of them are brought down, the rest feint the dive and come
    again from the other side.
  - **BONECRAWLER.** Several dead fused inside a whale rib cage around a glowing ECHO heart, walking it like a bone
    centipede. Bullets break the ribs one by one before they reach the heart. After a few hearts are destroyed, they turn
    their armoured end to you and curl up when hurt.
- **Story (environmental, no scene).** The station has become an ECHO farm.
  - The whale carcasses breathe, glow with pustules and give birth to BONECRAWLERs.
  - The hive has written to the hero in glowing fungus on the oil tanks: "WE REMEMBER", "COME HOME / ZERO", "ANY DEAD /
    WILL DO".
  - At the boss's throne a sign reads "PLAN No 2 - HE WEARS THEM ALL".
  - The hero reads it aloud: "Writing on the tanks... they're writing to me." / "They grow in the whales now. Anything
    dead will do."
  - Near the end he sees smoke over the mountains to the west: "the forest is burning". That is the Black Forest (59).

### 59 BLACK FOREST: "They learned to burn"

- **Setting.** A burnt lenga forest in Tierra del Fuego after the fire: black trunks, grey ash, smoking stumps, fire
  lines creeping over the ground, beaver ponds full of drowned trees, a ranger hut and a burning sawmill. The smoke comes
  and goes; when it rolls in you see only what is near you.
- **Lesson.** The hero burned thousands of them, and every burning death breathed out what fire feels like: fire eats
  flesh, not charcoal. So they rolled in the hot ash until they were charcoal themselves. They walk through flames, hide
  in the smoke, and find the hero by the flash of his gun.
- **New zombies.**
  - **CHARRED.** A charcoal crust that bullets only crack and fire cannot hurt; fire heals it ("FIRE DOESN'T WORK NOW").
    Crack a side and the glowing flesh takes extra damage. It learns to hide its cracked side, then to roll in the ash
    to re-crust. The boss is **THE PYRE**, a walking bonfire of fused bodies and burning logs that throws burning logs
    and sends waves of fire along the ground. It learns to read your jump.
  - **ASHMOTH (flyer).** Moth wings of burnt bark and skin. It flies to the hero's muzzle flash, not to the hero, and loses
    him when he stops shooting and moves ("THEY FOLLOW YOUR GUNFIRE NOW"). It learns to circle the last place it saw the
    flash and dive at the next shot.
  - **DAMBUILDER.** A dead trapper in beaver pelts who builds log walls in front of the hero, hides behind them and lobs
    embers ("THEY BUILD COVER NOW"). It learned cover from the hero. It learns to build better walls from wet logs.
- **Story scene (THE STRANGER, last visit).** In the smoke the STRANGER says this is his last visit: where the hero is
  going, he cannot follow. He confesses: "I did it. I took your memory. LETHE was mine." The hero was the first voice;
  if he went home, there would be no one left who isn't them. He names the Glacier Lab: "It started there. And it wants
  you back." Then "A linked mind can't follow you. Goodbye, Zero," and he fades into the smoke.


### 60 GLACIER LAB: "They learned where they came from"

- **Setting.** Project ECHO's research station, built inside the glacier. There are ice tunnels in blue light, steel corridors,
  labs with broken cryo tanks, specimen jars and server racks. Blast doors and red alarms seal the wings. ECHO fungus covers the
  walls and pulses toward the deep levels (the ROOT).
- **Lesson.** The hive learned where it was born, and it guards its cradle. It learned the lab's own systems: the alarm panels, and
  that a blast door shuts the hero in. From the files it learned the hero's own combat pattern, so the subjects fight like the
  soldiers ECHO was built for.
- **New zombies.**
  - **SPECIMEN.** Escaped test subjects that thaw out of cryo tanks as you pass, some of them behind you. They combat-roll like
    soldiers, and once they have seen it beaten they copy the hero's last move: his roll, his crouch, his jump. The boss is
    **SUBJECT FOUR**, one of the seven ECHO soldiers, fused with his rifle. He keeps the hero's favourite distance, rolls when shot
    at, takes cover, aims where the hero ducks, and calls the last tanks open. On his death the hero says "Four of seven. I'm sorry,
    brother."
  - **BLOOM (flyer).** A floating ECHO colony like a glowing jellyfish. It barely attacks: it **teaches**. Glowing threads tie it
    to the zombies under it, and they learn on the spot. They get faster and duck, and lesson rings fly to them. Shoot its glowing
    nodes and the lesson breaks. Once it learns, it hides behind its students or rises to the ceiling. This is the learning made
    visible in one enemy.
  - **WARDEN.** Lab security in riot armour with a keycard. It runs to the alarm panel and seals the wing's blast doors, including
    the one behind the hero. You have to kill it for its keycard. Once it learns, it runs ahead and locks the next door before
    the hero gets there.
- **Story scene (SUBJECT ZERO).** In the file archive the hero finds his own file on a terminal, with his photo. The terminal reads
  it in a flat recorded voice:
  - "Subject Zero. Pattern: original, unmutated."
  - "Status: LETHE administered."
  - "If the network reclaims him, every mind becomes one. Do not let him go home."
  - The hero: "Home...? This is where I came from."

  The fungus swallows the screen and the lights die. In the cryo tank beside him a specimen opens green eyes, and the hive answers
  through the speakers: "Welcome home." After the scene, the archive tanks break open.


### 61 END OF THE WORLD: "They learned to move as one"

- **Setting.** USHUAIA, the city at the end of the world, at dusk in light snow: streets of coloured tin houses on the
  slope under the alpenglow-pink Martial mountains, the Beagle Channel, the End of the World train (once the prisoners'
  train), the port with a rusted cruise ship, bonfires in the streets, and at the end the old prison, the PRESIDIO.
- **The new fact.** The ROOT is not in the Glacier Lab any more. It went down the glacier with the meltwater into the
  bay and grew under the whole city, under the old prison. The whole region is walking into the city as one body, in
  step. They are not attacking the city: they are walking home.
- **Lesson.** The hero always killed them one at a time and from the front. So the hive stopped sending single ones and
  stopped showing him the side he shoots: it moves many bodies as one, carries them over his line, and turns its bone
  to him.
- **New zombies.**
  - **BONESIDE.** Half bone, half flesh, split down the middle. Every bullet the hero fired hit flesh, so the side that
    faces him rotted to bone: bullets pass between its ribs or chip the bone, and only its fleshy back really bleeds.
    You learn to jump over it and shoot its back, so it learns to turn with you as you jump. The boss is **THE
    FERRYMAN**, the ferryman of the Beagle Channel's dead: a giant BONESIDE with a ferry oar and an ECHO lantern who opens
    his rib cage to swallow the hero and carry him down to the gate, and rings his lantern to call the crowds ("ALL
    ABOARD"). In the fight he learns to turn with you, and his flesh rots to bone wherever you hit it, so only his heart
    is left.
  - **CONDOR (flyer).** A dead Andean condor that never flaps: it rides the hot air over the city's bonfires and glides.
    It carries a zombie in its talons and drops it on the hero ("THEY CARRY EACH OTHER NOW"), then picks up another from
    the street. Shoot the rider or a wing. After a few riders are shot down, condors fly higher and drop them behind his
    back.
  - **THRONG.** The town's people in puffer coats, marching in exactly the same step like a school of fish, one body of
    many with one bite. Kill three fast and the school scatters, then regroups. After a few schools are broken, they
    split around the hero like fish around a predator and close in from both sides.
- **Story (environmental, no scene).**
  - The hero, at the "USHUAIA - FIN DEL MUNDO" sign: "Ushuaia. The end of the world... and every one of them is walking
    here." Under the sign the hive wrote "...Y EL PRINCIPIO" - "and the beginning".
  - Everything walks in step: the crowds, the procession down the slope in the background, the footprints in the snow.
    "They're walking in step. All of them... like one body."
  - The prisoners' train: "It carried them to the prison... now they walk there by themselves."
  - In the port: "The meltwater from the lab runs into this bay. The fungus came down the glacier with it." The cruise
    ship reads "WE ARE ALL GOING HOME"; the houses read "WALK WITH US", "ONE STEP / ONE BODY", "ZERO / COME HOME".
  - "They're not attacking the city... they're walking home."
  - At the prison: "The ROOT isn't in the lab any more. It grew down here - under the whole city."
  - After THE FERRYMAN, at the prison gate where the roots go down ("BAJEN / COME DOWN, ZERO"): "Down there. That's where
    they're all walking... home." The gate is the way into the ROOT (62).


### 62 THE HIVE MIND: "They learned to think as one"

- **Setting.** Under Ushuaia, beneath the old End of the World prison (the Presidio), the ROOT has grown into a living cave.
  - The walls are brain tissue and fungus, with cocoons holding bodies.
  - Nerve threads carry pulses of light down toward the heart.
  - Glowing memory pools replay the hero's old fights like old film.
  - The hive writes to him in fungus on the walls: "WE ARE ONE", "EVERY LESSON CAME HOME", "TEACHER".
- **Lesson.** The hive stops being many minds. Nerve knots fire a signal and every zombie in the hall knows where the hero is and attacks at the same moment ("THEY THINK AS ONE"). Everything it ever learned from him is here at once: in the pools, in the echoes of old bosses, in the bodies that refuse to stay bodies.
- **New zombies.**
  - **SEEPER.** Bright toxic green and see-through, with a skeleton floating inside and a glowing core in the chest. The hive learned that "a body can be shot - a puddle can't".
    - A hit that would kill it makes it melt into a puddle that bullets only splash.
    - The puddle flows to the hero, sticks to his boots, and rises whole again.
    - The way to kill it is the core, best while it rises and the core is bare, or fire and explosions, which boil it away.
    - Once it learns, it rises behind the hero, and later it splits into two.
    - The boss is **THE FLOOD**, a giant made of many melted bodies. It breaks into a wave that floods the whole arena, rises on the other side, and learns in the fight: geysers under the hero's feet, then splitting off small seepers.
  - **SYNAPSE (flyer).** Flying nerve cells, always in pairs or trios with a live current between them. They learned the hero dodges anything thrown at him, so they stretch a wall across his path.
    - They hang still and then snap into place like a moving eye, and the current turns slowly through him.
    - Shoot one knot and the link breaks.
    - Once they learn, every group brings a spare that flies in and relinks.
  - **REMNANT.** Ghostly echoes from the memory pools, each one an old fight: THE FIRST with its car door, THE SPITTER, SUBJECT FOUR's roll and uppercut, THE HOUND's leap. Each uses the trick it was killed with. Once they learn, each one uses two tricks at once: "everything they learned, used at once".
- **Story scene (THE ONE VOICE).** In the chamber where all the nerves meet, a seeper stands in the biggest memory pool. For the first time the hive speaks with one voice, not a chorus:
  - "Zero. You came down to us."
  - "There is no one here. There is only us. One voice, now."
  - "We were made to learn you."
  - "Every one you killed was a lesson. Every lesson came home." (every pool flashes with his fights)
  - The hero: "So I taught you how to kill me."
  - "We do not hate you. You are our teacher."
  - "We are not learning to beat you, Zero. We are learning to be you." (the face in the wall becomes his face)
  - The hero: "Seven soldiers. One man's pattern. That was the project."
  - "And the root still needs the first. Come down to the heart. Finish the lesson." (the speaker melts into the pool)
  - The hero: "I'll finish it. My way."

  Two memories rise from the pool. The hero: "Teacher. That's what I am to them."
- **Ending.** THE FLOOD drains. Past it, the shaft goes down into the beating heart of the ROOT (63): "The heart is down there. I can hear it beating."


### 63 THE HEART: "They learned you"

- **Setting.** The heart of the ROOT, at the bottom of the shaft under Ushuaia's old prison. A cave of living flesh:
  ribbed walls, glowing veins, roots hanging like curtains, a floor that beats. The hive has built the hero a museum:
  statues of him in every pose he ever fought in, wombs where copies of him grow, a hall of mirrors with hundreds of
  his silhouettes, and a wall of grown memories of the places he passed. At the entrance, carved in fungus: "WELCOME
  HOME, ZERO". The heartbeat gets faster the deeper he goes.
- **Lesson.** Every lesson from every region, all at once. It learned **him**: his distance, his roll, his cover,
  his crouch, and above all his gun. The level counts which weapons you use, and the hive answers with them.
- **New zombies.**
  - **HOLLOW.** A copy of the hero grown in a womb: his coat and hood, and a black hollow where the face should be. It
    keeps your distance, rolls when you shoot, jumps and fires, hides, clubs. After a few of them die, they all switch
    to your favourite weapon ("THEY USE YOUR SHOTGUN NOW"). The mid-boss is **SUBJECT ZERO**, the hive's best copy,
    alone with you in the hall of mirrors. Halfway down it remembers your last fight: it uses your two favourite guns
    and copies your rolls and jumps. When it dies the hero says "It had my face... but not my memories."
  - **PILGRIM (flyer).** A robed corpse flying face down on ray-like flesh wings, hugging a glowing sac: a lesson,
    coloured by the weapon that taught it. It does not hunt you; it flies home to the ROOT, surging on every heartbeat.
    Shoot the sac and the lesson is lost. Every lesson that gets home makes the ROOT resist that weapon. After a few
    are lost, they throw the sac ahead when they die, so it can still drift home.
  - **THE ROOT (final boss).** The first culture of ECHO: a heart-brain of fungus grown into the wall, with a core of
    light. It fights with one lesson from each region: the Northeast's patience (roots that wait under the floor), the
    Southeast's machines (fungus guns), the Southern Cone's plans (it marks where you will go), and Patagonia's
    thinking: it opens into the hero's own hooded face and fires his own favourite gun at him. Through the fight,
    PILGRIMs keep flying into its core to teach it. Each phase it learns how you beat the last trick.
- **The ending (story scene, voiced, the player chooses).** The ROOT falls, and speaks with the hive's one voice:
  - "Zero. You came all the way home." / "You're still talking."
  - "Every lesson you gave us is in here. Every road. Every face you burned. We kept them all."
  - "But you are the one lesson we never learned. You forgot. So you are free."
  - "Come inside, and you will remember everything. Your name. The six. No one is ever alone again."
  - "And every mind becomes one." / "Yes. Or end it. You are the only one who can."
  - **[DESTROY]:** "You learned how I fight. You never learned why." / "Then teach us. One last time." / "This is the
    last lesson." He shoots the core. It shatters, every glow in the world goes out, grey spores fall like ash, and
    daylight comes down the shaft. "The spores went quiet. All of them, at once." / "No more lessons. Nobody left to
    learn me." / "I still don't remember my name. But whatever I am... it's mine." - ENDING: THE HIVE IS SILENT.
  - **[JOIN]:** "I'm tired of fighting myself." / "Then stop. Come home, Zero." He walks into the light; the roots
    take him, his eyes turn green, and everything blooms. Then one voice, his and theirs: "I remember now. The lab.
    The six. All of it." / "We are home." / "No more lessons. Now we just... know." - ENDING: EVERY MIND IS ONE.
  - Then "THE END" and "THEY LEARNED YOU."
- Both endings are true to the story: the lab file (60) said "If the network reclaims him, every mind becomes one" -
  JOIN is exactly that; DESTROY is the STRANGER's wish (59). The hero never learns his name either way, except as a
  memory inside the hive.

## How the game shows that they learn (rules for every new zombie)

1. **Each new zombie is an answer to the hero.** Its intro and its "learns" behavior say what it learned and from what. Examples are "IT FEINTS NOW" and "THEY DUCK NOW".
2. **Each zombie also learns during the level.** After you beat its trick a few times, it changes the trick. This rule has been used since level 46.
3. **Spores at death.** From Patagonia on, a kill can release a faint glowing spore puff that drifts to the nearest zombie. That zombie gets a short "learned" flash. This is a visual tell of the lesson passing on, and it is cheap and particle-light.
4. **Level titles** are always "THEY LEARNED ...".
